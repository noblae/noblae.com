the gym at five in the morning

Nobody is there to be seen. The lights hum, the mats still smell like yesterday, and the only sound is a heavy bag swinging on its chain after someone has already left. Whatever you came to prove, there is no one to prove it to.

That is the point. Most of what we call discipline is really performance, effort that only counts if someone is watching. The early hour strips that away. What is left is the work itself, and the plain question of whether you will do it anyway.

I have come to think that character is mostly built in rooms like this: cold, empty, unremarkable. The fight is only the part other people see.
