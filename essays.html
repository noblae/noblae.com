9.11.2001

It was fate. It had all been fate. An event necessitated by causality: the attackers grew in a certain way with certain beliefs due to their shared cultural heritage which had been passed down, a moral system created by those whose values were reactions and responses to the specific challenges in their environment. The roots of this event go decades back, centuries back, millennia back, an eternity back: to the point beyond time, where did the reptile sliver out of the primordial pool and onto land, and when they did how hot was it when they got there.
