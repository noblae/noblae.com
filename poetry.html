pale white animal

11.16.25
I say that I was born two million years ago, maybe longer ago than that. This is what I am. I am white and pale and I was worshipped as a god before civilization existed. I have existed since time immemorial. I am an anachronism, something hitherto unexplained language is a tool and it remains insufficient, though beautiful, in its attempts to explain my nature. I am alive and have been alive for eternity. the cave drawings depicting me as a god four hundred thousand years ago the sighting of me at the beginning and end of a battle in 1400 BC the ramblings and diary entries of a British explorer who went mad No one has pierced into the utter simplicity of my being I am older than time I am older than the existence of language, the dawn of consciousness and logical thinking. The creation of buildings, the discovery of fire. I am no god. I have no name. Not an unutterable name, but no name at all. I cannot be tamed. I cannot be dominated. I am present within your blood, your muscle, your bones. I have been present at the dusk and dawn of every war the site of every genocide, every holocaust I have walked along the sands of every battlefield I have existed for all of eternity And I will never die.
