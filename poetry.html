conversation on a metro

04.30.26
I remember you you were younger then and you smiled more. I remember you what happened to you? You were still a little boy. I remember when you were in high school and you came and visited us you seemed so sweet, so kind and warm. What happened to you? I notice you don’t talk much anymore. I noticed that you seem quieter less confident more distant less sure of yourself. Different. You seem different, like something fundamental something huge, something within your personality has changed, slipped away. The easy charm, the laughter. It seems gone. And, looking at you now it’s hard to see the same person that I once knew. I want to repeat the question again, because in those green eyes something is calling out to me something pained, some hurt, something inside you is talking to me without words I’ll repeat the question because I want to know: what happened to the boy I once knew
